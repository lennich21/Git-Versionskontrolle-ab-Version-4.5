A short summary of the work should be given. This should not exceed one page. The relevant aspects that should be addressed are:

\begin{enumerate}
	\item What is the problem/motivation addressed in this work?
	\item Why is the problem relevant?
	\item What is the current state of the art / why has the problem not been addressed so far?
	\item What is your solution? 
	\item What are the main results and contributions of your work that addressed the original problem
\end{enumerate}

To get an orientation how an abstract can look like, you can take a look into published scientific papers or dissertations.
