This is the IEEM LaTeX template for student projects, bachelor, and master theses as well as dissertations of our HKA-internal PhDs. The template includes a consistent layout and chapters for each variant. You can configure the meta data in the main LaTeX file. Four examples ("Example_Bachelor.pdf", ...) are given in the examples folder. You will find that titlepages, meta chapters, etc. change, depending on the configuration and language that you choose. The most part of that is automated. This repository also includes the "Hinweise Abschlussarbeiten ..." from Prof. Arnemann and Dr. Sommers :wink: "Foliensatz - Wissenschaftliches Arbeiten". Feel free to play around with the configurations and review the documents for bugs. The plan is to distribute the template to our students, so we have a uniform document structure for our projects and theses.
